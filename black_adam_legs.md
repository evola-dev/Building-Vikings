# Black Adam Workout — #BuildingBlackAdam

> Fonte: Instagram @daverienzi

---

## 🦵 LEGS

### Abduction Machine
- 3 Sets | 10 Reps w/ 2 sec. peak contraction hold | Rest 30 seconds

### Barbell Hip Thrust or Hip Thrust Machine
- 3 Sets | 10 Reps w/ 2 sec. hold at top | Rest 90 seconds

### Leg Extension
- 4 Sets | 25 Reps Total | Rest 60 seconds
  - 10 Reps 3 sec. negatives
  - 10 Reps 2 sec. hold at top
  - 5 Regular reps

### TRI-SET — 4 Rounds
- Rest 2 mins. 30 sec. between tri-sets
  - Leg Press: 20 Reps Total · 10 Reps 3 sec. negatives · 10 Regular reps
  - Pulse Walking Lunges w/ Dumbbells (1 pulse per step): 24 steps
  - Goblet Squat: 20 Reps

### Safety Bar Squat or Barbell Back Squat
- 4 Sets | 10 Reps w/ 4 sec. negatives and 2 sec. hold at bottom | Rest 90 seconds

### Seated Leg Curls
- 4 Sets | 12 Reps w/ 2 sec. hold at bottom | Rest 30 seconds
