# Black Adam Chest Workout

## Seated Cable Fly
- 4 sets
- 12 reps total
- Rest: 30 seconds

Breakdown:
- 4 reps 3-sec negatives
- 4 reps 3-second static hold in the middle
- 4 regular reps

## Flat Dumbbell Bench Press
- 4 sets
- 20 reps
- Rest: 90 seconds

## Incline Plate Loaded Bench Press w/ 3-sec Negatives
- 4 sets
- 8 reps
- Rest: 2 minutes

## Incline Smith Machine Bench Press w/ 2-sec Hold at Bottom
- 4 sets
- 12 reps
- Rest: 90 seconds

## Tri-Set (1 set = 3 consecutive exercises below)
- 3 sets
- Rest: 90 seconds

Exercises:
1. Flat dumbbell flyes — 8 reps
2. Neutral grip dumbbell bench press — 8 reps
3. Dips — to failure
