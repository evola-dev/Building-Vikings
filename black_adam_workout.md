# Black Adam Workout — #BuildingBlackAdam

> Fonte: Instagram @daverienzi

---

## 💪 SHOULDERS

### Reverse Fly Machine
- 4 Sets | 12 Reps Total | Rest 30 seconds
  - 4 Reps 3-sec negatives
  - 4 Reps 3-sec hold at top
  - 4 Regular reps

### Dumbbell Bent-Over Lateral Raise w/ 2-sec Hold at Top
- 4 Sets | 12 Reps | Rest 60 seconds

### Cable Single-Arm Side Lateral Raise w/ 3-sec Negatives
- 4 Sets | 8 Reps | Rest 60 seconds

### Dumbbell Side Lateral Raise
- 4 Sets | 12 Reps each arm | 15 Reps Total | Rest 60 seconds
  - Alternating from a static hold, 3 reps at a time
  - 3 Reps x both arms

### SUPERSET
- 3 Sets | Rest 60 seconds
  - Dumbbell Overhead Press | 20 Reps
  - Scaption Raises | 12 Reps w/ 2-sec hold at top

---

## 💪 ARMS — Biceps

### Barbell Curls
- 4 Sets | 20 Reps | Rest 30 seconds

### Dumbbell Concentration Curls
- 4 Sets | 12 Reps Total | Rest 60 seconds
  - 4 x 3-sec negatives
  - 4 x 3-sec hold at top
  - 4 x Regular reps

### TRI-SET *(1 Set = 3 consecutive exercises below)*
- 4 Sets | 8 Reps each | Rest 90 seconds
  - Incline Dumbbell Curls (Alternating Static Hold)
  - Incline Hammer Curls
  - EZ Bar Spider Curls (2-sec hold at top)

---

## 💪 ARMS — Triceps

### Rope Pushdown
- 4 Sets | 20 Reps | Rest 30 seconds

### Dumbbell Skull-Crusher
- 4 Sets | 12 Reps Total | Rest 60 seconds
  - 4 x 3-sec negatives
  - 4 x Alternating static hold
  - 4 x Regular reps

### TRI-SET *(1 Set = 3 consecutive exercises below)*
- 4 Sets | 10 Reps each | Rest 90 seconds
  - Dual-Cable Pushdowns (Reverse Grip)
  - Alternating Static Hold
  - Dual-Cable Pushdowns (Overhand Grip)
  - Dumbbell Kickbacks (2-sec hold at top)
