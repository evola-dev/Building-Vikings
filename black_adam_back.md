# Black Adam Workout — #BuildingBlackAdam

> Fonte: Instagram @daverienzi · 10 novembre 2022

---

## 💪 BACK

### Reverse Grip Pulldown
- 4 Sets | 12 Reps Total | Rest 30 seconds
  - 4 Reps 3-sec negatives
  - 4 Reps 3-sec static hold
  - 4 Regular reps

### Plate Loaded High Row w/ 2-sec Hold at Bottom
- 3 Sets | 8 Reps | Rest 60 seconds

### T-Bar Rows
- 4 Sets | 20 Reps | Rest 90 seconds

### Plate Loaded Mid Row
- 4 Sets | 12 Reps Total | Rest 2 minutes
  - 8 Reps alternating static hold
  - 4 Regular reps

### Wide Grip Pull-Ups
- 4 Sets to failure w/ 3-sec negatives each rep | Rest 2 minutes

### TRI-SET *(1 Set = 3 consecutive exercises below)*
- 3 Sets | 8 Reps each | Rest 2 minutes
  - Cable Row (Reverse Grip)
  - Cable Row (Overhand Grip)
  - Stiff Arm Rope Pullover
