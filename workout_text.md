# Workout Routine

## 1️⃣ Four sets of...

- Tricep press downs (30,20, 10, 10 drop 10)  
Each set immediately into...
- Spider curls (15, 12, 10, 10 drop 10)
- Rest 90 seconds between supersets

## 2️⃣ Four sets of...

- 10 reps each of one rolling tricep extension into one strict skull crusher into one close grip press.  
Each set immediately into...
- 10 single arm bicep curls each arm while holding the opposite arm in contraction immediately into 10 double arm curls
- Rest 90 seconds between supersets

## 3️⃣ Four sets of...

- 10 double contraction dips (halfway up into one full rep) Each rep with a 3 second negative down immediately into...
- 10 cheat curls with a 3 second negative down
- Rest 90 seconds between supersets
